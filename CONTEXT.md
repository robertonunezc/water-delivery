# Water delivery credit

Credit sales retain their order identity and payment history when responsibility
for their outstanding debt moves between corporate and branch accounts.

## Language

**Order client**: The client or branch that placed the order. A debt transfer
does not change the order client.

**Credit account**: The corporate or branch account responsible for a credit
order's outstanding debt, with its own independent credit limit.

**Debt transfer**: Moving only the remaining unpaid credit debt from one credit
account to another, releasing the same amount on the source account.

**Original credit terms**: The payment conditions in effect when an order first
uses credit. Moving its debt to another account does not reset those conditions.
